\section{Conclusions}
An S-band power amplifier was designed, fabricated and characterized. The experimental results at 2.2\,GHz are aligned with the output power and efficiency requirements, although the measured response is centered at 2.11\,GHz and 2.2\,GHz lies outside the $S_{11}<-10$\,dB bandwidth. Including the resistor ESL in the design model is expected to correct this shift in a future revision. A favorable compromise was obtained between output power and linearity, with a P1dB of 30.3 dBm and an OIP3 of 39.83 dBm. The drain efficiency reached 32.94\% at an output power of 30.92 dBm. The design parameters are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:pa_results}.

The obtained output power and bandwidth validate the module for data transmission at a 2.96 Mbps rate with a BER lower than $10^{-5}$ for the downlink in LEO missions.

Finally, the correlation between experimental results and simulated values confirms the proposed design methodology, consolidating a development and characterization platform for RF modules that can be reused in future aerospace applications.
